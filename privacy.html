Privacy Policy

Rosary Atelier, by Golden Pearl LLC. Last updated November 20, 2026.

Rosary Atelier is a prayer app. It was built to be quiet, and that includes how it treats your information.

No account, no sign-in

Rosary Atelier does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information to use the app.

Your prayer stays on your device

Your prayer progress and the rosaries you save in your Rosary Box are stored on your own device. They are not sent to us.

No advertising, no tracking

Rosary Atelier contains no advertising and no advertising trackers. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share personal data with third parties for marketing.

Email you send us

If you write to us for support, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and we use them only to reply to you.

Children

Rosary Atelier is suitable for all ages and does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
