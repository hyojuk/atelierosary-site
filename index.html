Rosary Atelier

A quiet, beautiful place to pray the Rosary. Each day opens with the Church's calendar and the day's Gospel, beneath sacred artwork chosen for the season.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Pray, bead by bead

Begin where you left off, or start anew. Opening prayers and all four sets of Mysteries guide you through the Rosary, one bead at a time.

Today

The day's Gospel and the liturgical calendar, with sacred artwork chosen for the season you are praying in.

Guided Mysteries

Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious, and Luminous. A guided Rosary that remembers your place, so you can return and resume.

Create

Build a rosary of your own, and keep the rosaries you love in your Rosary Box, ready for the next prayer.

Sacred artwork

Every piece in the app carries its story with it. Tap any artwork to meet the artist and see its source on its own page.

Two lights to pray in

Night, in midnight navy and antique gold. Lourdes, in ivory and blue.

Rosary Atelier was made slowly, and made with love, by a Catholic family, for anyone who wants a calmer way to keep a daily Rosary.
