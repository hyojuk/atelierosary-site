About us

We are Claire and Charlie, a Catholic family in Washington. Before there was an app, there were rosaries made at our own table, by hand, for our own prayer and for the people we love.

We built Rosary Atelier to carry that same care into an app, a quiet place to pray the daily Rosary, with the day's Gospel, the Mysteries, and sacred artwork, made as personally as the rosaries we make by hand.
